A little privacy note

Your bike details and precise coordinates are stored only in this browser’s local storage. There is no account or cloud synchronisation in this version. Anyone using this browser profile may see them.

Map tiles are provided by OpenStreetMap; its servers receive your IP address and the map area you view. Leaflet loads from unpkg. Directions open Google Maps and share the chosen destination with it.

The app shell works offline after a successful first visit over HTTPS or localhost. The map and directions require an internet connection. We do not download maps for offline use.

Backups contain exact locations. Keep them private and outside your public repository.
